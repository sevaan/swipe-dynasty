Five ways to play a life

The same opening life, Aru on a cold evening, in five layouts. Play a few cards of each and see which one feels right in the hand.

  1. 1The CardOne tactile card you deal, drag, flick and flip.
  2. 2The NotebookThe inventor's journal: ink, paper, a taped-in sketch, page turns.
  3. 3CinemaThe picture fills the screen; the words play like subtitles.
  4. 4ChatThe scene arrives as messages; you reply with one of two answers.
  5. 5Big TypeEditorial and bold: huge words, big answer rows, swipe the page.

These are feel prototypes, not the game: each plays one life and loops. Back to the game
